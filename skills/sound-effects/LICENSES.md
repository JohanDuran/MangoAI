Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, public domain). Commercial use and redistribution are
allowed and credit is not required, but is appreciated.

- Interface Sounds — https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

`catalog.json` lists every file with its source pack. Any sound you add must be CC0 or have a
license that permits redistribution in this repository; record it in the catalog's `source`
field.
